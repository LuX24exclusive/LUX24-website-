About LUX24

A small start with a bigger marketplace in mind.

LUX24 is an independent business exploring a marketplace model for international products. The initial phase is intentionally simple: source selected products through Indian suppliers, test demand, understand unit economics, and build a reliable assortment.

Over time, LUX24 can expand into a platform where verified importers, wholesalers and retailers can present products they already stock, while LUX24 focuses on discovery and online demand.

Important: LUX24 is independent and is not Red Bull GmbH or an authorised Red Bull distributor. Product availability, pricing, sourcing and marketplace approval are subject to supplier and platform verification. Brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
